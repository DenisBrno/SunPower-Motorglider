# SunPower Motorglider

An autonomous, long-endurance solar-powered glider designed for seasonal operation (September–October) in the Czech Republic. Featuring a **3.2-meter wingspan**, the aircraft combines lightweight 3D-printed structures with an optimized solar-battery hybrid powertrain to achieve all-day flight from dawn to dusk.

---

## 🚀 Key Specifications

* **Wingspan:** 3,200 mm 
* **Target Weight:** ~3250 g 
* **Airfoil:** AG35 
* **Wing Area:** ~70 dm² 
* **Wing Loading:** 46.5 g/dm² (at 1,500 g) 
* **Stall Speed:** ~8.1 m/s 
* **Control Surfaces:** Elevator + Rudder

---

## 🔋 Power & Energy Strategy

* **Solar Array:** 28× Sunpower C60 cells.
* **Battery Pack:** 3S5P Li-Ion (15× Li-Ion 50E EVE 5000mAh 3,6V 21700, 270 Wh, 1050 g).
* **Propulsion:** An XM3844EG-12 DUAL ENGINE brushless motor paired with an GM 14x8F Folding Flat propeller, optimized for high efficiency at low thrust.

---

## 🛠️ Airframe & Manufacturing

* **Fuselage & Tail:** 3D-printed using lightweight, active-foaming **LW-ASA** filament to cut standard material density nearly in half (~0.5–0.6 g/cm³).
* **Wing Structure:** LW-ASA 3D-printed spiral vase rib construction.
* **Spar System:** $\emptyset$10-8 mm carbon tubes for the main wing spar and a $\emptyset$8 mm tube for the tail boom.
